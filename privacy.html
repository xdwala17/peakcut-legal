Privacy Policy for PeakCut

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Introduction

PeakCut is a desktop video editing and clipping application.

Information We Process

Depending on features used, PeakCut may process video files, audio files, transcripts, URLs, project information and application settings.

AI Processing

When AI features are enabled, relevant content may be sent to the configured AI provider, such as Google Gemini.

Third-Party Services

PeakCut may integrate with Google Gemini, YouTube and TikTok. These services have their own privacy policies.

Account Connections

When you connect an account, authorization information may be processed to provide the requested features.

Data Storage and Security

Some project information and files may be stored locally on your computer. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with providers when needed to operate features you choose.
